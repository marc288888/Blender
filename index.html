📘 The whole book is in here

Nothing has been left out. The program follows the KG3 PHONICS book in the book's own order, so a child can practise on the computer exactly what the class is doing in print.

  • 46 units — all 26 letters, the digraphs ch, sh, th, wh, and all 16 blending sounds.
  • 188 picture words — the book's own vocabulary, with a picture for every word.
  • 46 stories — built from the book's tongue-twisters and sentences.

A child who has done a page in the book meets the same letters, words and pictures here — just in a game they can play on their own.

🕹️ Driving with the joystick

  1. Hold the red stick and push it left, right, up or down. The orange ring hops that way.
  2. Press the green button. Whatever the ring is sitting on opens.
  3. ◀ Back (or Escape) steps back.

Why a joystick? Young children's hand muscles are still developing, so aiming a mouse and clicking at the same time is hard for them. This joystick was built for them: a big stick to push and one big button to press — nothing to aim at, and each move clicks so they can feel it.

🔡 Choosing a letter

The first screen has two big buttons at the top, the same two parts as the KG3 PHONICS book: Letters a–z (a to z plus ch, sh, th, wh) and Blending sounds (ag, am, an, ap, at, ar, ed/eg, en, et, er, in, og, op, ot, ub/up, ug).

  • Drive to a letter and press the button.
  • Each letter has three activities, in this order: Story → Find → Make, plus extra games: Match, Word, Picture, Sound and Memory.
  • Finish the Make game and the letter earns a ⭐ on the grid, so you can see at a glance where a child is up to.

Stars are saved on this computer. You can clear them any time in Teacher Settings.

📖 1. Story

  • You read it out loud. There is no recorded voice on purpose — your voice teaches better than a machine.
  • The red words carry the letter sound. Stretch them: “a… a… ant”.
  • Every page is one whole picture of what is happening, so children follow the story before they can read it.
  • Joystick left / right turns the page. The last page leads straight into Find.

👆 2. Find

  • Pictures appear. The child picks every one that has the sound in it.
  • The counter shows how many are still to find.
  • A wrong pick just wobbles. Nothing is lost, no sad noise, nobody fails.

Ask the child to say the word out loud before pressing. That is where the listening happens.

🔤 3. Make

A picture, a row of empty boxes, and the letters jumbled underneath. The child puts them in the right order.

  • Drag — hold the button on a letter, drive it to a box, let go.
  • Pick up & drop — press once to lift a letter, drive to a box, press again to drop it. No holding needed.
  • Digraphs stay together on one tile, so sh‑i‑p is three tiles, not four.

🔗 4. Match

Four pictures from the book page across the top, their words jumbled underneath. The child joins each picture to its word.

  • Press the button on a picture — it lights up and the ring jumps down to the words.
  • Press the button on its word. Right: a line joins them and the word is read out. Wrong: it just wobbles.
  • Starting from a word works too. Play again deals new pictures.

The words are not read out until they are matched, so the child has to read them.

🎲 More games

  • 📝 Word — one big picture, three words. Pick the word that goes with the picture.
  • 🖼️ Picture — one word, three pictures. Read the word, pick its picture. 🔊 reads the word out if a child needs help.
  • 👂 Sound — three pictures, only one has the sound (“Which picture starts with c?”).
  • 🃏 Memory — eight cards face down. Turn two at a time to find each picture and its word.

A wrong pick just wobbles. Each finished game earns a ⭐, then Play again deals new pictures.

⚙️ Teacher Settings

Two ways in, both made for adults:

  • Hold the ⚙ in the corner for 1 second.
  • Or press Ctrl + Shift + S — instant, and easiest with a joystick.

A quick click does nothing, so a child cannot wander in by accident.

Inside you can set the number of spare letters in the tray, how many pictures Find shows, lowercase or UPPERCASE, word labels, dwell select and its speed, the computer voice (off by default), and Reset stars.

Teacher keys: Ctrl+Shift+S settings · Ctrl+Shift+Q quit · F11 fullscreen.

⏱️ A 15 minute lesson

  1. Warm up — 2 min. Say the sound together three times. Make it big: “sss… snake!”
  2. Story — 5 min. Read it slowly. Point at the picture and ask “what can you see?” before you turn the page.
  3. Find — 3 min. Make them say the word before they press the button.
  4. Make — 5 min. Sound each letter as it drops into its box, then blend the whole word together.

One letter a day works well. Keep Friday for going back over the letters already starred.

Teacher Settings

Computer voice

Off by default — you read the story and the sounds yourself. When it is on, the computer sounds out letters and words in the games, and a 🔊 Read to me button appears on the story pages. Pick a Thai voice (e.g. Premwadee, Niwat) to hear the English read with a Thai accent.

Joystick: use Arrow Key Mode for the simplest control, or Mouse Mode with dwell select for children who cannot aim and click at the same time. Teacher keys: Ctrl+Shift+S open these settings, Ctrl+Shift+Q quit, F11 fullscreen.
